About

My name is Arshaq Mohamed. I design and build websites that make businesses look like the leaders in their field — and turn their visitors into customers. Premium work, at prices that make sense.

Many projects begin with a reference — a website you admire, a screenshot or a rough sketch. I replicate its look and feel, then tailor it to your brand, your content and your customers. If you'd rather start fresh, I design something original from the ground up.

I care about the details you feel without noticing — how a page loads, how a button answers your tap, how it reads on a phone in bright sunlight. And launch isn't goodbye: I stay on after your site goes live to keep it updated and running.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Can you copy a website I like?

    Yes. Send me the link and I'll replicate the look and feel — the layout, the motion, the mood — rebuilt with your brand, your content and your own images.

    What kind of websites do you build?

    Business and corporate sites, online stores, landing pages, restaurant and café sites, booking sites, portfolios, and web apps like dashboards and internal tools. If it runs in a browser, I can build it.

    Will my site work well on phones?

    Yes. Every site is designed for phones from the start and tested on real devices, so it's fast and easy to use on any screen.

    Can my website be in Arabic and English?

    Yes — with proper right-to-left layouts for Arabic, not just translated text.

    What happens after my site goes live?

    I don't disappear. Updates and changes after launch are part of the service, so your site stays current as your business grows.

    How do we get started?

    Message me on WhatsApp or email with what you have in mind — a reference, a sketch or just an idea. I'll reply with questions, a plan and a price.
